## Starting point

Read this for the distinction between dynamic and kinematic controllers, then follow its scene setup and physics-process movement example.

## Constraints and compatibility

The conceptual term kinematic does not imply using a Godot 3 KinematicBody API. This snapshot uses Godot 4 CharacterBody2D. Use the CharacterBody guide for current movement-method details and collision response.
