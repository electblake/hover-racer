## Starting point

Read the quick start, then the timing introduction and usage guide. Continue to advanced interpolation for cameras and to the 2D/3D comparison for coverage limits.

## Constraints and compatibility

Interpolation smooths presentation between fixed simulation ticks. It is not an increase in physics accuracy or a remedy for missed collisions.
