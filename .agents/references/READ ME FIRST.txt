FOR BLAKE - HOVER RACER

QUICK START: Extract this ZIP and open PLAY GAME.html in a browser supporting
WebGL 2. Everything is inline. No downloads or Godot runtime are needed.
Click the game or press a driving key to enable wind audio.
Controller button opens connection and live input testing. Connect by USB or
pair in system Bluetooth, then press a controller button for browser detection.
W/S throttle/brake; A/D and arrows steer; Q/E sharp turns; R reset.
Controller: RT gas, LT brake, both sticks steer, inside stick down sharpens turns.
