##  Every vehicle in Star Wars Galactic Racer 

 Star Wars Galactic Racer ships 50 vehicles split across its four classes: 12 speeder bikes, 12 landspeeders, 12 skim speeders and 14 podracers. Each one is a kit, which is the name the game gives to a complete set of body parts in a single style, and every kit here carries four studio renders and an assembled 3D model you can turn in the browser. 

 Most kits cost 100 Kit Points at the Scrap Dealer and the rest are handed to you by the campaign, by a racer you beat or by the edition you bought. The vehicle page for each one names the exact condition.

##  Do vehicles have different stats in Star Wars Galactic Racer? 

 Across the Speeder Bike, Landspeeder and Skim Speeder classes, no. All 36 of those kits in Star Wars Galactic Racer fill the same six body part slots, 30 of them cost 100 Kit Points and 31 of them are worth 180 Kit Points in body parts. A speeder bike handles like a speeder bike whichever body you hang on it. 

 Performance in Star Wars Galactic Racer comes from three places instead. The vehicle class sets the handling model and names the advanced stat. The six upgrade tracks raise Ramjet, Afterburner, Cornering, Resilience, Battery and that advanced stat. Vehicle parts, which are the mods bought with credits rather than Kit Points, add their effects on top. Body parts change nothing but the look. 

##  The podracers with a trait of their own in Star Wars Galactic Racer 

 Podracers are the exception, and the only place in Star Wars Galactic Racer where the vehicle itself changes performance. Six of the 14 carry a signature Legendary trait that cannot be bought or moved to another vehicle, each one paired with a Fault it brings along. 

 Dud Bolt's Podracer runs Optimized Nozzles for more Afterburner power and top speed on straights, against Draggy Control Surfaces. Gasgano's Podracer runs Energy Surplus for Afterburner power and tank refill rate, against a Large Cross-Section. Teemto Pagalies's Podracer runs Bleeding-Edge Tech for Afterburner power and Cornering, against Small Thrusters. Ben Quadinaros's Podracer runs Double Control Surfaces for less drag and better Cornering, against Complex Interlocks. Ody Mandrell's Podracer runs Overbuilt Engines for a higher top speed, against Massive Momentum. Sebulba's Podracer runs Built for Battle for less damage taken and more damage dealt in collisions, against Old Dog, Old Tricks. 

 The remaining podracers, Shade's Podracer in its three phases and Kestar Bool's Podracer, carry no trait of their own. Podracers are also not bought: they belong to the racers who fly them, and several appear a second time as an aged variant. Their 134 body parts carry no unlock text at all, because nothing in the game grants them separately. 

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many vehicles are there in Star Wars Galactic Racer?

There are 50 vehicles in Star Wars Galactic Racer, split into 12 speeder bikes, 12 landspeeders, 12 skim speeders and 14 podracers.

### Do vehicles have different stats in Star Wars Galactic Racer?

Not within the Speeder Bike, Landspeeder and Skim Speeder classes. Those 36 kits share the same slots and the same cost, and performance comes from the vehicle class, the six upgrade tracks and the vehicle parts you install. Podracers are the exception: six of them carry a signature Legendary trait and a matching Fault.

### Do body parts change performance in Star Wars Galactic Racer?

No. Body parts are cosmetic and are bought with Kit Points. The parts that change how a vehicle performs are vehicle parts, bought with credits, and any of them fits any vehicle of the right class.

### Which podracers have their own trait in Star Wars Galactic Racer?

Six of them: Dud Bolt's (Optimized Nozzles), Gasgano's (Energy Surplus), Teemto Pagalies's (Bleeding-Edge Tech), Ben Quadinaros's (Double Control Surfaces), Ody Mandrell's (Overbuilt Engines) and Sebulba's (Built for Battle). Each one also carries a Fault that comes with the trait.

### How much does a vehicle cost in Star Wars Galactic Racer?

Most kits cost 100 Kit Points. The body parts that make one up are 30 Kit Points each, so buying a kit piece by piece runs to 180 Kit Points for a standard vehicle.

### Can you see the 3D models from Star Wars Galactic Racer?

Yes. Every one of the 50 vehicle pages has the assembled model in a viewer you can rotate and zoom, alongside three-quarter, side, front and top renders.
