#  Star Wars Galactic Racer Glossary 

 Every keyword and status effect in Star Wars Galactic Racer, from Ramjet and Afterburner to Corroded and System Malfunction, with the icon the game shows and what it does. 

## [Ability](https://galacticracer.gameslantern.com/glossary/ability)

Equippable tech that provides offensive, defensive, or utility capabilites. Consume Energy to activate.

##  Every keyword in Star Wars Galactic Racer 

 Star Wars Galactic Racer leans on 55 keywords, and they turn up in the text of nearly every ability, part and trait in the game. Ramjet is the boost that overheats, Afterburner the safe one that refills, Cooled slows the heat, Burning speeds it up, and Surging, Overcharged and Shielded are the states worth chasing. 

 Thirteen of them are status effects, which means another racer can put them on you. Corroded, Frozen, Concussed, Choked, Disrupted and System Malfunction are the ones to watch for, and each says exactly what it does to your vehicle.

 Part and ability descriptions in Star Wars Galactic Racer are written in keyword shorthand, so a line like "Cooled is more potent" only means something once you know Cooled slows Ramjet heat. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Ramjet in Star Wars Galactic Racer?

Ramjet is the powerful boost that builds heat as you hold it. Let the heat run away and the vehicle explodes, so managing it is the core skill of the game.

### What is the difference between Ramjet and Afterburner in Star Wars Galactic Racer?

Ramjet is the dangerous boost that overheats and has to be managed. Afterburner is the safe boost that refills from a tank while you race.

### What status effects are in Star Wars Galactic Racer?

Thirteen, including Burning, Cooled, Chilled, Frozen, Concussed, Choked, Corroded, Disrupted, Shielded, Surge, Blinded, Overclocked and System Malfunction.
