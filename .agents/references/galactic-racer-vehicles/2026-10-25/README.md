## Provenance and scope

StarWars.com and the game's own website are official product sources. Xbox Wire is an official platform publication; its June preview describes hands-on experience, not a physics specification. Games Lantern is an independent community reference, not an official developer source.

The saved pages are HTML-to-Markdown text mirrors with source URLs and retrieval metadata. Images, interactive 3D models, embedded videos, and linked detail pages are not downloaded. Vehicle and class listings remain useful entry points for further research.
